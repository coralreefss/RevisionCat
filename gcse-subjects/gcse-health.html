Top Tips:

· Learn the terminology.

· Use real-life examples to make your answers more specific.

· Know the factors affecting development and how they can interact with one another.

· Learn life stages in order and understand how people develop across them.

· Don't just describe: explain why something affects someone's health or development.

· Practise applying knowledge to scenarios rather than only revising definitions.

· Know the different types of care and support and when they may be appropriate.

· Use case studies to help identify needs and recommend appropriate support.

Grade 8/9 Tips:

Below, you can find a few tips to help you achieve a grade 8 or 9 ( equivalent of A or A*) in Health and Social Care!

· Always apply your answer to the person in the scenario.

· Use precise terminology naturally, do not force it.

· When evaluating a choice, discuss both advantages and disadvantages before reaching a judgement.

· Consider how the same factor can affect people differently.

· Link different areas of development together: physical, intellectual, emotional and social factors can interact.

· Use specific examples rather than saying something is simply "good for health".

Must-Know Information:

Below, you can find a small checklist of must-know information for GCSE Health and Social Care. The specific content, terminology and legislation you need to learn will depend on your qualification and exam board.

· The meaning of health and wellbeing and the different dimensions of health.

· The physical, intellectual, emotional and social dimensions of development.

· The main stages of the human life course.

· Physical, intellectual, emotional and social development across different life stages.

· Factors that can affect human growth, development, health and wellbeing.

· The impact of relationships, lifestyle, environment and life events on individuals.

· The importance of a balanced diet, physical activity and healthy lifestyle choices.

· Physical health conditions and their possible effects on an individual's wellbeing.

· Mental health and factors that can affect emotional wellbeing.

· The different types of care and support available to individuals.

· The roles and responsibilities of health and social care professionals.

· The importance of communication in health and social care settings.

· Different communication methods and how they can be adapted to meet individual needs.

· The importance of respecting an individual's rights, dignity, privacy and choices.

· Equality, diversity and inclusion in health and social care.

· Discrimination and the different ways it can affect individuals.

· Safeguarding and the importance of protecting vulnerable individuals.

· Confidentiality and the circumstances in which information may need to be shared.

· The importance of person-centred care.

· The principles of effective and respectful care.

· Relevant legislation, policies and professional responsibilities required by your course.

· The purpose and importance of different health and social care services.

· How individuals can access health and social care services.

· Barriers that can prevent people from accessing appropriate care and support.

· How health and social care professionals can help overcome barriers.

· How to apply knowledge to health and social care scenarios.

· How to explain the effects of factors on an individual's health, development and wellbeing.

· How to evaluate different care options and reach a justified conclusion.
