Top 3 Resources:

Below, you can find my top 3 resources for GCSE Dance revision. These are the resources I found through research as I did not take GCSE Dance. However, these are just my recommendations and opinions based off of what I found. You may find other resources more useful for your revision!

1. Simple Study

Simple Study has revision notes, quizzes, flashcards, exam questions and past papers. The current AQA Dance material is organised by individual topics and anthology works.

2. Rambert Classroom

Rambert Classroom is really good for Dance specifically. Rambert has GCSE resources including material for A Linha Curva, with interviews, motif videos and information about the work. Some resources are free with an account.

3. BBC Bitesize

BBC Bitesize provides notes, video explanations and quizzes to help you revise and understand content. Bitesize also explains certain dance techniques such as travelling and jumping.

YouTube Channels:

If you're looking for some Youtube channels to help you revise, below are a few I found through research!

1. Rambert

Rambert , as mentioned above, is particularly useful for contemporary dance and set-work material. They have behind the scenes videos as well as a 40-video AQA playlist of movement content.

2. National Theatre

National Theatre is good for seeing professional performance and developing understanding of performance choices. Some of their playlists also include Vocal Training and Learning.

3. Stopgap Dance Company

Stopgap Dance Company may be useful for contemporary dance and "Artificial Things" if that's one of your set works.

For Dance, it is recommended that you prioritise watching actual performances over generic "revision videos". Watching a professional performance and then analysing the actions, space, dynamics, relationships, costume, lighting, music etc. is much more useful than just watching someone talk about Dance for 20 minutes.

Top Tips:

· Know your terminology. Don't just say "they move quickly." Say fast pace, accelerated movement, etc.

· Watch your set works repeatedly, and analyse them if necessary.

· Always connect choices to meaning/intent.

· Watch professional performances.

· Know your own choreography inside out. Be able to explain why you chose particular movements, music, staging and choreographic devices.

Grade 8/9 Tips:

Below, you can find a few tips to help you achieve a grade 8 or 9 ( equivalent of A or A*) in Dance!

· When analysing choreography, connect action, space, dynamics and relationships rather than discussing each completely separately.

· Explain intent, choreographic choice, and then audience effect.

· For choreography questions, think like a choreographer, not just a dancer. Explain why you'd make the choice.

Must-Know Information:

Below, you can find a small checklist of must-know information for GCSE Dance. These topics are commonly tested in the exams and may get you a few easy marks.

· The six choreographic processes: improvisation, selection, repetition, development, manipulation and structuring.

· Actions, space, dynamics and relationships.

· Choreographic devices such as motif, repetition, canon, unison, contrast, highlights and climax.

· Physical skills such as posture, alignment, balance, coordination, flexibility, strength, stamina and control.

· Technical skills such as timing, accuracy, extension, spatial awareness and musicality.

· Expressive skills such as projection, focus, phrasing and communication.

· How lighting, costume, set, props and aural setting contribute to a performance.

· Your set works/anthology: choreographer, company, stimulus, intent, style, structure and key movements.

· How to analyse a dance using action, space, dynamics and relationships.

· How to explain the effect of a choreographic or production choice on the audience.
