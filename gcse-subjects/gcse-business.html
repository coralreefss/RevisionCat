Top 3 Resources:

Below, you can find my top 3 resources for GCSE Business revision. These are the resources I found most useful when revising for my exams. However, these are just my recommendations and opinions based off of what I found most useful. You may find other resources more useful for your revision!

1. Save My Exams

Save My Exams was my personal favourite website for GCSE Businesss. Once I had learnt the content through flashcards, I used SaveMyExams to find past papers and mark schemes.

2. Flashcards

Usually, I recommend websites or apps here. Instead, I want to recommend flashcards. Flashcards was one of the most useful techniques to revising Business. Since this subeject is mostly memorisation, flashcards and spaced repetition was most useful to remember the content, for me. My subject teacher was most helpful for learning exam technique. For reference, I did OCR.

3. BBC Bitesize

BBC Bitesize provides notes, video explanations and quizzes to help you revise and understand content. There is also a section on exam technique for each exam board! Personally, mastering exam technique was the most difficult part of the course.

Top Tips:

· Know your formulas AND what the result means. If you're doing OCR like I did, here is a list of all the finance formulas you need for the course: click here!

· Practice calculations regularly. Most of them are quite simple, but easy to forget.

· Always apply your answer to the business in the question.

· For longer questions, always consider both sides.

· Use chains of reasoning. For example, you could try: Point → Because → Therefore → This means.

Grade 8/9 Tips:

Below, you can find a few tips to help you achieve a grade 8 or 9 ( equivalent of A or A*) in Business!

· Don't stop at "this will increase profit." Explain why. For example, increased sales means higher revenue. Assuming costs don't rise by the same amount, this could lead to higher profit.

· Use the information in the case study. If the business is new, talk about the lack of brand recognition.

· Consider the size and type of business. One decision might be great for a large PLC, but not for a new sole trader.

· Make a clear judgement. For OCR especially, there are actual marks for saying which decision you agree with. Then extra for explaining your choice.

Must-Know Information:

Below, you can find a small checklist of must-know information for GCSE Business. These topics are commonly tested in the exams and may get you a few easy marks.

· The difference between primary, secondary and tertiary sectors.

· The difference between sole traders, partnerships, private limited companies and public limited companies. And a few advantages and disadvantages of each.

· Entrepreneurial characteristics and why people start businesses.

· Market research: primary vs secondary, qualitative vs quantitative.

· Market segmentation and the different ways a market can be segmented.

· The marketing mix (4Ps): product, price, place and promotion. And how they influence each other.

· Pricing strategies and when each might be appropriate.

· Revenue, costs, profit and loss calculations.

· Break-even, including calculating and interpreting the break-even point.

· Cash flow: inflows, outflows, net cash flow and closing balance.

· Sources of finance and the advantages/disadvantages of each.

· Motivation and methods of motivating employees.

· Economies and diseconomies of scale

· Production methods and productivity.
