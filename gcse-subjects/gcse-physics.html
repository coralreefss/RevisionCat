Top 3 Resources:

Below, you can find my top 3 resources for GCSE Physics. These are the resources I found most useful when revising for my exams. However, these are just my recommendations and opinions based off of what I found most useful. You may find other resources more useful for your revision!

1. Cognito

Cognito was one of my personal favourite websites for GCSE Physics. They provide clear animated explanations, quizzes and exam questions. Cognito is brilliant for learning difficult concepts without making them unnecessarily complicated.

2. Physics and Maths Tutor (PMT)

PMT was a very helpful resource too. Topic by topic questions, notes, past papers and mark schemes. Particularly useful once you've learnt the content.

3. FreeScienceLessons

FreeScienceLessons provides short, specification-focused videos covering GCSE Physics topics. His videos may be slightly dull, but he covers the content accurately and quickly.

Top Tips:

· Understand the physics rather than memorising equations. Know what each quantity actually represents.

· Practise rearranging equations.

· Always include units in calculations.

· Learn your required equations and know when to use each one.

· Practise graph questions: gradients, relationships, anomalies and interpreting data.

· Know your required practicals: method, variables, measurements, sources of error and improvements.

· Do lots of exam questions. Physics questions often apply familiar equations/concepts in different situations.

Grade 8/9 Tips:

Below, you can find a few tips to help you achieve a grade 8 or 9 ( equivalent of A or A*) in Physics!

· Learn the difference between precision, accuracy, repeatability and reproducibility.

· When explaining something, use a proper chain of reasoning rather than listing disconnected facts.

· Practise questions where you have to combine multiple topics.

· When you get a question wrong, don't just write the correct answer. Work out where your method went wrong.

· Do mixed-topic papers regularly.

Must-Know Information:

Below, you can find a small checklist of must-know information for GCSE Physics. These topics are commonly tested in the exams and may get you a few easy marks.

· The difference between scalar and vector quantities.

· Distance, displacement, speed, velocity and acceleration.

· The equations for motion and how to rearrange them.

· Forces, resultant forces and Newton's laws.

· Work done, energy and power.

· Energy stores, transfers and efficiency.

· Momentum and conservation of momentum.

· Density, pressure and moments.

· Waves, including wavelength, frequency, amplitude and wave speed.

· Reflection, refraction and the electromagnetic spectrum.

· Electric circuits, including current, potential difference and resistance.

· The differences between series and parallel circuits.

· Electrical power, energy and efficiency.

· Magnetism, electromagnetism and the motor effect.

· The particle model, including density, internal energy and changes of state.

· Atomic structure, isotopes and the different types of nuclear radiation.

· Alpha, beta and gamma radiation and their properties.

· Half-life and nuclear equations.

· Nuclear fission and fusion.

· Required practicals and how to evaluate experimental methods and results.
