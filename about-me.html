About Me!

Hi! I'm Coral, the creator and developer of RevisionCat. Due to my love of computers and Computer Science, I decided I wanted to make a website. During the summer of Year 11, after my exams, I taught myself HTML and CSS using a 6.5 hour course on YouTube. Following that, I started creating RevisionCat on the 17th of July 2026.

Currently, I am so proud of RevisionCat as it is something I planned and created myself, and it was something I committed to as a long-term project. I hope to keep updating it in the future!

Why did I want to create RevisionCat?

I wanted to create RevisionCat to help other students during exams. My hope is that my website can help revision seem less overwhelming and provide some support and resources to students that may need it. RevisionCat covers individual subjects to Managing Exam Stress and How to Revise, as they were things that myself and/or my friends struggled with during GCSEs. This is also why I added a Microsoft Form for students to anonymously submit any other questions they have. I hope to review this regularly and update my website in the FAQ sections.

About my website:

Everything has been created by me. I designed the logos and icons on IbisPaint, programmed everything myself using VSCode, and spent hours designing and developing my website. I researched all information myself and wrote it into my own words. Additionally, I avoided using AI (unless you count the Google AI overview). I'm hoping to continue updating it with new content, especially once I also learn JavaScript.
